UNIK LABS SITE — INTEGRATED PROTOTYPE

Start here:
1. Open index.html in a web browser.
2. Choose Create with AI or Upload artwork.
3. Complete either flow and add the configured product to the shared cart.
4. Review the cart and place a prototype order.

Included pages:
- index.html — landing page
- studio.html — UNIK Labs AI Design Studio
- upload.html — custom artwork upload and garment placement
- checkout.html — shared cart, delivery form, and local order confirmation
- account.html — member sign-in, order history, and AI generation archive
- calibrate-hoodie-front.html — interactive hoodie front print-zone calibrator
- calibrate-hoodie-back.html — interactive hoodie back print-zone calibrator

Hoodie front calibration:
1. Open calibrate-hoodie-front.html.
2. Drag and resize the red print zone over the hoodie.
3. Use the four ratio fields for fine adjustment.
4. Choose Apply to custom upload to use the calibration on this browser, or
   Copy measurements to share the exact values for a permanent code update.

Use calibrate-hoodie-back.html for the same process on the back of each hoodie
colour. Front and back values are stored and applied independently.

Prototype notes:
- The cart and prototype orders are stored only in the current browser/device.
- Member profiles and history are also device-local in this prototype. Passwords
  are never stored. Production launch needs secure server-backed authentication.
- The light/dark appearance is saved per browser. Product and model imagery
  remains in its original supplied form in both modes.
- Finished AI and custom-upload mockups are carried into cart, checkout, and
  member order history as visual previews.
- No card or payment details are collected and no real payment is taken.
- A production launch still needs a payment provider, server-side order storage,
  real AI image generation, email notifications, and fulfilment integration.
- Some previews and typefaces load from the internet.
