What we review

Personal, responsible decisions.

Identity & address

Your ID, live selfie, residential details and proof of address help us confirm your identity and prevent duplicate or fraudulent applications.

Affordability

Recent bank statements help us understand regular income, essential commitments and whether a payment plan is manageable for you.

Your options

If Pay Later is not available, you can appeal, use SETLA Laybuy, or submit a new application after three months.
